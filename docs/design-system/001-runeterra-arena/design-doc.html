Reference research

Observed Visual Patterns

The reference site uses a fixed black navigation bar around 80px tall, uppercase navigation labels, circular utility icons, a bright cyan play action, and a large cinematic hero with a centered mark, short uppercase copy, and a gold primary button. The page then moves into large editorial sections, carousels, split content blocks, and a dense institutional footer.

The Patch 26.11 article adds a long-form editorial pattern: a centered article header with title, playful subtitle, category, authors, and date; a narrative intro; highlights; then repeated sections for Pride 2026, ranked client changes, route missions, support adjustments, champions, items, runes, ARAM, Arena, bug fixes, future skins, chromas, and related articles.

On mobile, the brand and utility icons remain in the top bar, navigation collapses, hero content stacks vertically, the primary button becomes full width, and editorial blocks become a single-column feed.

Design direction

Visual Personality

The system should feel cinematic, competitive, premium, and direct. Use dark surfaces as the stage, gold for prestige and primary calls to action, cyan for active digital affordances, and crimson only for destructive or urgent states. The interface should be dramatic in hero areas and disciplined in functional UI.

Body copy should be concise and never compete with media or actions. Use uppercase text for navigation, buttons, badges, tabs, and short labels only.

Patch notes article pattern

Game Update Content

Use this pattern for detailed updates that mix editorial commentary with dense balance data. Keep the article body narrow, separate each gameplay area with H2 headings, and make each repeated change entry scannable through an icon, entity name, designer note, and short change list.

Accessibility

Usage Guidelines

Maintain strong contrast on dark surfaces, preserve visible focus rings, and avoid relying on gold alone to communicate state. Keep all interactive targets at least 44px tall, use semantic buttons and links, and ensure mobile full-width buttons have concise labels.

Responsive behavior

Breakpoints

At widths below 900px, split sections collapse to one column and sticky documentation navigation becomes static. Below 760px, primary navigation is hidden behind a menu pattern and actions become full width when placed in clusters. Patch note articles should use a narrow single column, compact metadata, shorter section spacing, and one-column change entries. Below 520px, page gutters reduce to 12px per side and vertical rhythm tightens.
